# Abyss Roadmap

This document outlines the major versions of Abyss, past and planned. Only major (x.0.0) versions are listed here. Smaller updates and hotfixes are tracked using the following versioning scheme:

`release.smallupdate.hotfix`

For example, `3.1.0` would represent a small update to version 3.0 (such as a handful of bug fixes), while `4.2.1` would represent a hotfix to version 4.2 (such as patching an undetected crash or minor bug).


| Version | Name | Progress | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.0 | Foundation | Released | The initial creation of the Abyss dimension. Establishes the flat seabed terrain, removes caves and ravines, and sets a high sea level with a tall water column. |
| 2.0 | Not Alone | Released | Adds points of interest to the dimension, including a seabase and multiple submarine wreck variants, along with commands for visibility and manual structure placement. |
| 3.0 | Laying Foundations | In Progress | Introduces a set of custom building blocks for the Abyss, with the possibility of adding more content alongside them. |
| 4.0 | Deeper and Darker | Planned | Adds a proper natural entry method into the Abyss, including a change to how falling into the void in the End behaves, along with any other features added along the way. Subject to change |
| 5.0 | Building Pressure | Planned | Adds a custom oxygenation and drowning system. Below sea level in the dimension, players will begin drowning unless they are on a stable Create contraption. Also introduces a system where enclosed spaces (up to 25000 blocks, sealed off from the outside by waterloggable blocks) have their water replaced with a special breathable air. |
